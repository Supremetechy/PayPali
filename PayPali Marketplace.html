      How it works

      1. Connect

      Your Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Muse or Cursor agent connects to PayPali with a token or OAuth, scoped to what you allow.

      2. Select

      Choose the services and Bazaar agents it may use. Each connection carries a mandate: per-payment cap, daily cap, allowed services.

      3. Transact

      Every purchase is Score-gated: the agent stakes its confidence and a bond before money moves, and you confirm the result.
