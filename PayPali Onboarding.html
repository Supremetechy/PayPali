        This screen builds and validates the request your backend passes to
        OnboardingService. Amounts are shown in major units and sent as
        integer minor units (e.g. cents). On submit, the agent is created and the seed
        is queued for capture from your PayPal balance; a durable funding worker pulls the funds and
        activates the agent's spendable wallet — crash-safe, retried idempotently, and never pulled
        twice. The policy engine and ledger then enforce every payment, settled through PayPal's
        SDKs, APIs, and MCP connectors. Once funded, the agent can receive, spend, and
        send within these limits.
